1. Introduction & Scope of Policy

Welcome to Satvik Swaad ("we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to crafting authentic, artisanal Indian homemade pickles, murabbas, laddus, and traditional preserves prepared with pure cold-pressed mustard oil and traditional recipes.

This Privacy Policy describes our active data processing practices, technical safeguards, and privacy commitments for all visitors and customers accessing our website. This policy applies exclusively to information collected or processed through the Satvik Swaad customer platform.

2. Information Customers Provide

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when interacting with our storefront, submitting customer service messages, registering an account, or submitting product reviews.

  • Contact Inquiries: Your name, email address, phone number, inquiry subject, and message content submitted through our Contact form.
  • Account Authentication: Email address and credentials when creating or accessing an account.
  • Product Reviews: Author name/alias, star ratings, and review feedback submitted for public display.

3. Authentication & Account Information

Customer identity authentication is powered by secure Single Sign-On (SSO) and OAuth 2.0 protocols. Passwords are never stored in plain text by our application; they are securely hashed and managed directly by identity authentication services.

When you authenticate, session tokens (JSON Web Tokens / ID tokens) are issued and stored locally in your browser's persistent storage to maintain your login session. Administrative capabilities are strictly guarded by server-authoritative custom claims assigned by the system.

4. Contact Form Information

Messages submitted through our Contact Page are transmitted to our centralized backend service (`/api/v1/messages`) and stored securely in our encrypted customer database.

5. Order & Transaction Data

When customer order functions are active, order records containing customer shipping address, phone number, and purchased items are generated and processed via authoritative server functions.

6. Technical Information & Security Logs

Our web servers automatically record standard HTTP access request metadata, including IP addresses, user-agent strings, request timestamps, and HTTP response codes. These security logs are collected for rate limiting, DDoS defense, and security threat detection.

7. Cookies & Local Browser Storage

We use standard browser storage mechanisms (`localStorage`) to preserve client-side session state, cart items, and user UI preferences locally on your browser. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising pixels.

8. How Information is Used

Information collected is utilized exclusively for: (a) authenticating your user session, (b) processing and fulfilling your support requests, (c) displaying approved product reviews, and (d) enforcing rate limits and platform security controls.

9. Legal & Operational Processing Grounds

Our processing of personal data is grounded in: (a) your consent when voluntarily submitting contact forms or reviews, (b) performance of contract/service when creating an account, and (c) our legitimate interest in maintaining platform security, rate limiting, and technical stability.

10. Cloud Infrastructure & Hosting

Our platform is hosted on high-availability cloud infrastructure, and database persistence is managed by an encrypted cloud database. Strict server-side security rules and access control policies prevent unauthorized access or direct database tampering.

11. Backend Application Processing

Application API logic runs on dedicated backend application servers, handling rate limiting, security validation, and server-side request routing.

12. Service Providers & Third-Party Processors

We engage trusted cloud infrastructure providers strictly to operate our platform:

  • Cloud Infrastructure Providers: Authentication, encrypted cloud database, and web application hosting.

13. Data Sharing & Non-Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data. Information is shared only with our core cloud infrastructure providers or if required by applicable law, court order, or governmental authority.

14. Data Retention Policies

Contact form submissions are retained in our database to manage support records and deleted upon customer request. Security audit logs are retained for compliance auditing. Local browser storage data remains under user control on your device.

15. Security Safeguards & Technical Limitations

We employ multi-layered technical security controls, including HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, attestation token verification, rate limiting, and strict server-authoritative custom claims authorization.

Realistic Security Acknowledgment: While we maintain rigorous technical protections, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely immune from all security threats. We strive to protect your personal information using industry best practices.

16. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for general consumer audiences. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from individuals under 18 years of age. If you believe a minor has submitted personal data to us, please contact us for deletion.

17. Customer Choices & Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable Indian data privacy principles (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data you have provided to us.

18. Account & Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of your contact messages or account data, submit a request through our Contact Page selecting "Data Request" as the subject. Data deletion requests are verified manually and processed by system administrators within 30 days.

19. Email & Communication Preferences

We send communications strictly in response to customer inquiries or transactional account notices. We do not send unsolicited marketing emails or sell your contact information to third-party advertisers.

20. Cross-Region Processing

Our primary business operations are located in India. Cloud hosting infrastructure may route or process data in secure data centers located in Asia, Europe, or the United States under standard security protocols.

21. Disabled & Future Functionality

To maintain full transparency, the following features remain disabled or deferred in our active codebase:

  • Online Commerce & Payments: Checkout operations are disabled (`COMMERCE_ENABLED=false`); no online transactions occur.
  • Cloud Storage Uploads: File and image uploads are deferred due to billing configuration.
  • Marketplace Synchronization: External marketplace integrations are disabled (`MARKETPLACE_AMAZON_ENABLED=false`).

22. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" revision date.

23. Contact Method for Privacy Questions

If you have questions, concerns, or data deletion requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us through our official Contact Page.
