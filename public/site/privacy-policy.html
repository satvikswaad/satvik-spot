Order Details & Information We Collect

At Satvik Swaad, we craft authentic, artisanal homemade Indian pickles, murabbas, and traditional sweets. We believe data collection should be as pure and unadulterated as our ingredients. We collect only the minimal personal information necessary to prepare, pack, and safely deliver your handcrafted jars to your home:

  • Delivery Information: Your full name, delivery address, postal PIN code, and landmark instructions to enable accurate, damage-free delivery by our courier partners.
  • Contact Credentials: Your mobile phone number and email address to send real-time order confirmation receipts, parcel dispatch notifications, courier tracking numbers (AWB), and delivery coordination alerts via SMS or WhatsApp.
  • Order History: The specific pickle varieties, weight variants (e.g. 250g, 500g, 1kg glass jars), and order timestamps so you can conveniently view your past purchases and reorder favorites within your account profile.
  • Customer Service Inquiries: Any questions, feedback, or delivery preferences you voluntarily provide when contacting our family support team via WhatsApp, email, or our Contact page.
📦 Minimal & Purposeful: We collect zero extraneous background data, unnecessary device permissions, or personal profiling metrics. Every piece of information collected serves a direct, transparent purpose: delivering fresh, handcrafted preserves to your table.
2

Payment Security & Bank-Grade Financial Protection

Your financial security and peace of mind are paramount. All transactions on Satvik Swaad are handled using industry-leading, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant financial infrastructure:

  • Bank-Grade 256-Bit Encryption: Online payments are processed through RBI-authorized payment gateways (including PayU and Razorpay). All payment details are transmitted over encrypted TLS/SSL connections directly to the payment processor.
  • Zero Card & PIN Storage: Satvik Swaad never views, handles, or stores your debit/credit card numbers, CVVs, expiry dates, net banking passwords, or UPI PINs on our servers. All sensitive financial authentication occurs exclusively on the gateway's secure, hardened banking servers.
  • Supported Payment Channels: We support secure payments across all major Indian platforms, including UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), RuPay, Visa, Mastercard, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD) in serviceable pin codes.
  • Automatic Banking Reversals: If a network failure occurs during checkout where your bank account is debited but an order confirmation fails to generate, your money is completely safe. The payment gateway's automated banking reconciliation protocols ensure 100% of the funds are reversed to your original source account within 3 to 7 working days.
🔒 100% Encrypted Transactions: Your checkout process is fully safeguarded by rigorous banking standards and tokenized authentication, ensuring zero risk of credential exposure.
