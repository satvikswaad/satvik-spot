1. Introduction & Scope of Policy

Welcome to Satvik Swaad ("we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to crafting authentic, artisanal Indian homemade pickles, murabbas, laddus, and traditional preserves prepared with pure cold-pressed mustard oil and traditional recipes.

This Privacy Policy describes our active data processing practices, technical safeguards, and privacy commitments for all visitors and customers accessing our website. This policy applies exclusively to information collected or processed through the Satvik Swaad customer platform.

2. Information Customers Provide

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when interacting with our storefront, submitting customer service messages, registering an account, or submitting product reviews.

  • Contact Inquiries: Your name, email address, phone number, inquiry subject, and message content submitted through our Contact form.
  • Account Authentication: Email address and password credentials when creating or accessing an account.
  • Product Reviews: Author name/alias, star ratings, and review feedback submitted for public display.

3. Authentication & Account Information

Customer identity authentication is powered by Firebase Authentication (a Google Cloud service). Passwords are never stored in plain text by our application; they are securely hashed and managed directly by Firebase's authentication infrastructure.

When you authenticate, session tokens (JSON Web Tokens / ID tokens) are issued and stored locally in your browser's persistent storage to maintain your login session. Administrative capabilities are strictly guarded by server-authoritative custom claims assigned by the system.

4. Contact Form Information

Messages submitted through our Contact Page are transmitted to our centralized backend service (`/api/v1/messages`) and stored in Cloud Firestore under the `'messages'` collection.

Contact submissions are processed exclusively to respond to customer questions, resolve product inquiries, and assist with privacy or data subject requests. Access to messages is restricted to authorized administrative personnel.

5. Order & Transaction Information

ℹ️ Current Operational Status: Online commerce, checkout, and payment processing are currently DISABLED (`COMMERCE_ENABLED=false`) pending completion of final business registrations (FSSAI and GSTIN).

During this preparatory phase, checkout operations cannot be completed and no financial transactions take place. When commercial operations are enabled in the future, order placement will process delivery address and contact information for order fulfillment.

Zero Payment Card Storage: Satvik Swaad does not collect, store, or process credit card numbers, debit card PINs, CVVs, or UPI banking credentials. Any future digital payments will be processed directly through PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment gateways.

6. Automatically Collected Technical Information

When you access our platform, technical diagnostics are automatically generated by network infrastructure and application servers for security and operational maintenance:

  • IP Address & Rate Limiting: Client IP addresses (`req.ip`) are evaluated in transient server memory to enforce API rate limits and prevent Denial-of-Service (DDoS) abuse.
  • HTTP Headers & User-Agent: Browser type, operating system, request timestamp, and requested URL paths are logged for server diagnostics.

7. Cookies & Browser Local Storage

Satvik Swaad uses browser LocalStorage and IndexedDB strictly for core functional features:

  • `satvik_cart`: Stores your selected shopping cart items locally on your device so your cart persists across page refreshes.
  • `firebase:authUser`: Stores encrypted session state locally when you sign into an account.

Zero Advertising or Tracking Cookies: We do not use third-party advertising cookies, behavioral tracking pixels, or cross-site tracking tools.

8. How Information is Used

We process collected information strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To receive, manage, and respond to your customer support inquiries.
  • To authenticate your identity and secure access to account services.
  • To enforce rate limits, detect fraudulent activity, and protect system infrastructure.
  • To moderate and display verified customer product reviews.

9. Legal & Operational Processing Grounds

Our processing of personal data is grounded in: (a) your consent when voluntarily submitting contact forms or reviews, (b) performance of contract/service when creating an account, and (c) our legitimate interest in maintaining platform security, rate limiting, and technical stability.

10. Firebase, Firestore & Cloud Infrastructure

Our static website is hosted on Firebase Hosting, and database persistence is managed by Cloud Firestore (Google Cloud Platform). Cloud Firestore enforces default-deny security rules (`firestore.rules`) to prevent unauthorized client access or direct database tampering.

11. Render Backend Application Processing

Application API logic runs on Render (Node.js Express backend). Render operates as our sole application server backend (`trust proxy = 1`), handling rate limiting, security validation, and server-side request routing.

12. Service Providers & Third-Party Processors

We engage trusted cloud infrastructure providers strictly to operate our platform:

  • Google Cloud / Firebase: Authentication, Cloud Firestore database, and Firebase Hosting.
  • Render Services Inc.: Express application backend server hosting.

13. Data Sharing & Non-Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data. Information is shared only with our core cloud infrastructure providers or if required by applicable law, court order, or governmental authority.

14. Data Retention Policies

Contact form submissions are retained in Cloud Firestore to manage support records and deleted upon customer request. Security audit logs are retained for compliance auditing. Local browser storage data remains under user control on your device.

15. Security Safeguards & Technical Limitations

We employ multi-layered technical security controls, including HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, Firebase App Check token verification, rate limiting, and strict server-authoritative custom claims authorization (`admin_owner`).

Realistic Security Acknowledgment: While we maintain rigorous technical protections, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely immune from all security threats. We strive to protect your personal information using industry best practices.

16. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for general consumer audiences. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from individuals under 18 years of age. If you believe a minor has submitted personal data to us, please contact us for deletion.

17. Customer Choices & Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable Indian data privacy principles (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data you have provided to us.

18. Account & Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of your contact messages or account data, submit a request through our Contact Page selecting "Data Request" as the subject. Data deletion requests are verified manually and processed by system administrators within 30 days.

19. Email & Communication Preferences

We send communications strictly in response to customer inquiries or transactional account notices. We do not send unsolicited marketing emails or sell your contact information to third-party advertisers.

20. Cross-Region Processing

Our primary business operations are located in India. Cloud hosting infrastructure provided by Google Cloud and Render may route or process data in data centers located in Asia, Europe, or the United States under standard security protocols.

21. Disabled & Future Functionality

To maintain full transparency, the following features remain disabled or deferred in our active codebase:

  • Online Commerce & Payments: Checkout operations are disabled (`COMMERCE_ENABLED=false`); no online transactions occur.
  • Firebase Storage: File and image uploads are deferred due to billing configuration.
  • Marketplace Synchronization: External marketplace integrations (Amazon SP-API / Flipkart) are disabled (`MARKETPLACE_AMAZON_ENABLED=false`).

22. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" revision date.

23. Contact Method for Privacy Questions

If you have questions, concerns, or data deletion requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us through our official Contact Page.
