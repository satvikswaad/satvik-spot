Safe Glass • Leakproof Transit

5-Layer Shatterproof Glass Packaging Protocol

Authentic Indian achar must breathe and age naturally in glass or earthenware — never in chemical-leaching plastic tubs. We bottle our preserves exclusively in premium, food-grade glass jars. Because glass demands extraordinary packaging care, every parcel is packed using our rigorous 5-layer protective protocol:

  1. Airtight Induction Seal: An aluminum induction foil seals the mouth of each jar to prevent oil seepage or pressure leakage during air or road transit.
  2. Tamper-Evident Security Band: The screw cap is shrink-wrapped with a security band confirming the jar has not been opened since leaving our kitchen.
  3. Multi-Layer Shock Bubble Cushion: Heavy-gauge bubble wrapping surrounds the circumference and base of every jar to absorb sudden impacts.
  4. Heavy-Duty Corrugated Carton: High-burst strength 5-ply cardboard boxes with internal dividers prevent jar-to-jar collision during transit.
  5. Fragile Handling Labels: High-visibility bright red "FRAGILE — GLASS & LIQUID" labels ensure couriers treat your box with utmost care.
