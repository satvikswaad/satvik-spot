1. Introduction

Welcome to Satvik Swaad ("we", "our", or "us"). We specialize in crafting authentic, artisanal Indian homemade pickles, murabbas, laddus, and traditional preserves prepared with pure cold-pressed mustard oil and traditional recipes.

This Shipping and Delivery Policy describes our current operational status, serviceability disclosures, and future logistics guidelines for customers visiting our platform.

2. Scope of this Policy

This policy applies to all visitors and customers browsing products on the Satvik Swaad platform. It governs disclosures regarding shipping, dispatch, physical delivery, and order fulfillment.

3. Current Commerce Status

ℹ️ Operational Notice: Online commercial transactions, checkout, and payment processing are currently DISABLED (`COMMERCE_ENABLED=false`). FSSAI Licence and GSTIN registrations remain pending regulatory approval.

The platform currently operates in informational preview mode. No financial transactions, sales contracts, or commercial purchases take place on the website at this time.

4. Current Online-Ordering Status

Online order placement is currently inactive. Order placement requests submitted to the backend API receive an automated status code 503 (`COMMERCE_NOT_AVAILABLE`). Online orders cannot be placed or completed through the storefront.

5. Current Shipping and Delivery Availability

Physical Delivery Inactive: Because online commerce is disabled, physical shipping, courier dispatch, and delivery services for online orders are not currently operational.

We are currently completing operational preparation and regulatory registrations before activating physical order dispatch.

6. Product Catalogue & Cart Limitations

Viewing product catalogue details (15 products) and adding items to your shopping cart (`satvik_cart`) are purely local to your browser and serve an informational purpose. Adding items to the shopping cart does not reserve physical stock, create an accepted order, or trigger courier dispatch.

7. Order Acceptance Status

No order is considered accepted, confirmed, or binding while online commerce is disabled. Form submissions or local cart selections do not constitute order acceptance by Satvik Swaad.

8. Future Serviceability & Delivery Coverage

When commercial ordering launches, physical delivery serviceability (serviceable PIN codes and geographic regions across India) will be published on our platform. Delivery coverage will depend on courier partner logistics.

9. Future Shipping Methods

Upon commercial activation, shipping options (such as standard ground shipping for glass-jar packaged pickles and preserves) will be announced. Express or same-day delivery promises are not currently offered.

10. Future Shipping Charges

Shipping fees and any free-delivery order thresholds will be explicitly calculated and displayed at checkout when commercial ordering is activated. No active shipping fees or free-shipping thresholds exist during the current preview phase.

11. Future Dispatch Processing

Our artisanal pickles and preserves are prepared in fresh weekly batches and sun-cured in pure cold-pressed mustard oil. Upon commercial launch, dispatch SLA processing times (batch curing and protective packaging) will be clearly communicated.

12. Future Delivery Estimates

Estimated delivery transit times will be provided by courier partners upon commercial activation. Any future delivery estimates will be approximate and subject to logistics carrier performance and destination region accessibility.

13. Future Order Tracking

Live order tracking features are currently inactive. When online sales open, shipment tracking details and courier airway bill (AWB) numbers will be provided for active orders.

14. Customer Shipping-Address Responsibilities

When commercial ordering is enabled, customers will be responsible for providing complete and accurate delivery addresses, including valid 6-digit Indian PIN codes and active contact phone numbers.

15. Address Changes

Guidelines for requesting address modifications prior to order dispatch will be established upon commercial launch. Address modifications cannot be guaranteed once a shipment has been handed over to a courier partner.

16. Unsuccessful Delivery Attempts

Procedures for handling returned shipments resulting from incorrect customer addresses, recipient unavailability, or repeated unsuccessful delivery attempts will be specified upon commercial activation.

17. Damaged, Missing, or Incorrect Shipments

Because our food products are packaged in protective glass jars, reporting protocols for transit damage or unboxing issues (such as submitting photos via our Contact Page) will be detailed when shipping begins.

18. Product Availability & Fulfilment Limitations

Our specialty products (such as raw mango pickles and seasonal amla murabbas) depend on seasonal harvest availability. Fulfilment capacity will be limited by raw ingredient seasonality and weekly batch curing limits.

19. Risk & Responsibility During Delivery

[Pending Owner & Legal Counsel Review] Terms regarding risk of loss and transfer of responsibility during courier transit will be finalized with legal counsel prior to activating commercial shipments.

20. Marketplace & Third-Party Fulfilment Status

External marketplace synchronization features (`MARKETPLACE_AMAZON_ENABLED=false`) are currently disabled. No shipments or orders are processed through Amazon FBA or Flipkart seller fulfillment at this time.

21. International Shipping Status

No International Shipping: Satvik Swaad does not offer cross-border or international shipping. Services will be restricted exclusively to domestic delivery within India when commercial sales open.

22. Relationship with Cancellation & Refund Policy

A dedicated Cancellation and Refund Policy will be published on our platform prior to activating online ordering. Cancellation rights, return eligibility for food items, and refund processing timelines will be governed by that policy.

23. Service Interruptions & Force Majeure

When physical shipping launches, dispatch and delivery schedules may be affected by events beyond reasonable control, including extreme weather events, natural disasters, courier transport strikes, or regional government restrictions.

24. Changes to this Policy

We reserve the right to update this Shipping and Delivery Policy to reflect operational updates or launch readiness. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

25. Contact Method

If you have questions regarding this Shipping and Delivery Policy or platform availability, please reach out to us through our official Contact Page.
