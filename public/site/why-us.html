Why Choose Satvik Swaad

We take immense pride in crafting authentic Indian pickles, murabbas, and traditional sweets without compromising on purity, ancestral recipes, or natural aging techniques.

☀️
Sun-Cured Maturity
Our pickles are matured under natural sunlight for days to allow spices and pure mustard oil to penetrate deep into raw mango, chillies, and amla.
🪔
Pure Cold-Pressed Mustard Oil
We use unrefined, cold-pressed mustard oil known for its natural preservative properties, pungent aroma, and authentic North Indian taste.
🌿
Zero Synthetic Preservatives
No vinegar, no chemical preservatives, and no artificial coloring agents. Only natural salt, oil, turmeric, and sun-drying.
👵
Ancestral Family Recipes
Crafted using whole hand-ground spices including saunf, kalonji, rai, hing, and roasted cumin following traditional household recipes.
