Micro-batches (< 50kg) sun-cured naturally in glass jars. Allows individual monitoring, gentle stirring, and unhurried natural curing.
Mechanized High-Heat
Continuous high-heat industrial kettle cooking & rapid mechanized packing. High-speed automated manufacturing lines to maximize unit throughput.
