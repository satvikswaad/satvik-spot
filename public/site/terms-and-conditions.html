Freshness • Dispatch

Ordering & Fresh Small-Batch Dispatch

Unlike mass-produced supermarket brands that store jars in dusty warehouses for months with chemical stabilizers, our products are cured and finished in weekly micro-batches:

  • Order Preparation: When you place an order on our website or via our verified WhatsApp concierge, your jar is inspected, sealed, and packaged freshly in our clean kitchen.
  • Dispatch Timeline: Orders are packaged and handed over to our trusted courier logistics partners within 24 to 48 business hours (excluding Sundays and national holidays).
  • Pre-Dispatch Cancellation: You can cancel your order free of charge within 2 hours of placing it or before courier pickup. Once your parcel is in courier transit, it cannot be recalled because perishable, artisanal foods cannot endure multiple transit return trips.
  • Address Accuracy: Please provide a complete delivery address with landmark, correct 6-digit PIN code, and an active mobile number to ensure seamless delivery by the courier executive.
📦 Micro-Batch Freshness: Because our kitchen produces limited jars per batch, seasonal specialties (such as winter Bharwa Lal Mirch or fresh Banarasi Amla Murabba) are offered while batch stock lasts.

100% Purity • Clear Pricing

Pure Ingredients & Transparent Pricing (100% Mustard Oil / A2 Ghee, All Prices in INR)

We maintain uncompromising transparency regarding everything that goes into our jars and our pricing structure:

  • Zero Palm Oil, Zero Artificial Preservatives: We prepare our savory achars exclusively in 100% pure cold-pressed Kachi Ghani mustard oil. Our traditional sweets are crafted in pure A2 cow ghee. We never use palm oil, cottonseed oil, synthetic vinegar (glacial acetic acid), or artificial chemical preservatives like Sodium Benzoate.
  • Sun-Cured Preservation: Our pickles preserve themselves naturally through ancient wisdom: the antibacterial qualities of pure mustard oil, Sendha Namak (rock salt), turmeric, and prolonged sun-curing under natural sunlight.
  • All Prices in Indian Rupees (INR, ₹): All prices displayed on our website are in INR and are fully inclusive of all applicable taxes. There are no hidden fees, dynamic surge fees, or unexpected payment markups.
  • Natural Artisanal Variations: Because we source real, non-GMO produce directly from regional farms (e.g. Ramkela raw mangoes, mountain amla, seasonal green chillies), minor variations in natural color, seed size, and tanginess are completely normal and testify to authentic, unadulterated artisanal preparation.
🌱 100% Satvik & Clean: Every product label lists the full ingredient spectrum with zero synthetic colors, zero artificial flavors, and zero hidden chemical enhancers.
4
Safe Glass • Transit Guarantee

Safe Glass-Jar Courier Delivery (Pan-India Transit Protection)

Authentic Indian achar must breathe and age in glass or ceramic — never in chemical-leaching plastic tubs. We package our entire collection exclusively in premium, food-grade glass jars.

  • Pan-India Courier Network: We ship across India via premier air and surface express couriers (Bluedart, Delhivery, DTDC, Xpressbees, India Post Speed Post). Most metro deliveries arrive within 3–5 business days, and regional deliveries within 5–7 business days.
  • Multi-Layer Shock Protection: Glass demands exceptional packaging care. Every single jar is individually vacuum-cushioned in heavy-duty bubble wrap, sealed in tamper-evident sleeves, and housed in rigid 5-ply corrugated outer boxes engineered to withstand long-distance transit.
  • 100% Free Transit Replacement Guarantee: In the rare and unfortunate event that a glass jar arrives cracked, chipped, or leaking due to mishandling by the courier, you are 100% covered. We will dispatch a brand new replacement jar immediately at zero extra cost to you.
  • Simple Claim Window: Simply share a clear photo or short unboxing video showing the damaged jar and outer shipping label on our WhatsApp (+91 92365 87600) within 48 hours of delivery. Our kitchen team will approve and ship your replacement without delay.
🚫 Food Safety & Consumable Nature: In compliance with FSSAI health and safety standards, opened or delivered food items cannot be returned for restock. Replacement is provided for transit damage or fulfillment errors.
