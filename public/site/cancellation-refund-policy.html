1. Introduction

Welcome to Satvik Swaad ("we", "our", or "us"). We specialize in crafting authentic, artisanal Indian homemade pickles, murabbas, laddus, and traditional preserves prepared with pure cold-pressed mustard oil and traditional family recipes.

This Cancellation and Refund Policy describes our current operational status, disclosures regarding cancellations, returns, replacements, and refunds, and customer inquiry procedures for our platform.

2. Scope of this Policy

This policy applies to all visitors and customers browsing products on the Satvik Swaad platform. It governs disclosures regarding order cancellations, product returns, item replacements, exchanges, and payment refunds.

3. Current Commerce Status

ℹ️ Operational Notice: Online commercial transactions, checkout, and payment processing are currently DISABLED (`COMMERCE_ENABLED=false`). FSSAI Licence and GSTIN registrations remain pending regulatory approval.

The platform currently operates in informational preview mode. No financial transactions, sales contracts, or commercial purchases take place on the website at this time.

4. Current Online-Ordering Status

Online order placement is currently inactive. Order creation requests submitted to the backend API receive an automated status code 503 (`COMMERCE_NOT_AVAILABLE`). Online orders cannot be placed or completed through the storefront.

5. Current Cancellation Availability

Online Cancellations Inactive: Because online commercial ordering is disabled, automated online order cancellation processing is not currently operational on the platform.

6. Current Return and Replacement Availability

Returns Inactive: Operational product return, replacement, and exchange services for online purchases are currently non-operational as no commercial orders exist.

7. Current Refund Availability

Refunds Inactive: Online refund processing and payment reversals are currently inactive (`PAYMENTS_ENABLED=false`). Zero financial funds or payment card details are collected or held by the platform.

8. Catalogue & Cart Limitations

Viewing product catalogue details (15 products) and adding items to your shopping cart (`satvik_cart`) are purely local to your browser and serve an informational purpose. Adding items to the cart does not reserve inventory, create an accepted order, or trigger payment processing.

9. Order Acceptance & Confirmation

No order is considered accepted, confirmed, or binding while online commerce is disabled. Form submissions or local cart selections do not constitute order acceptance by Satvik Swaad.

10. Future Customer Cancellation Requests

When commercial ordering is enabled, guidelines for customer-initiated cancellations prior to batch curing and dispatch will be published on the platform.

11. Future Seller-Initiated Cancellations

Upon commercial launch, Satvik Swaad reserves the right to cancel orders due to raw material unavailability, batch quality control rejection, unserviceable PIN codes, or pricing errors. Full refund rules for seller-cancelled orders will apply.

12. Future Cancellation Eligibility

Eligibility rules for canceling orders (e.g. before protective glass packaging and courier handover) will be specified prior to commercial sales activation.

13. Future Cancellation Deadlines

Specific cancellation cut-off timelines (e.g. prior to courier dispatch) will be announced when commercial checkout is activated. No active cancellation deadlines exist during the current preview phase.

14. Future Cancellation Charges or Deductions

Any cancellation fee structure or payment gateway processing fee deductions for customer-canceled orders will be published prior to commercial sales. No cancellation charges exist while commerce is disabled.

15. Future Return Eligibility

When commercial ordering launches, return eligibility conditions will be clearly defined. Because our food products are prepared without artificial preservatives, return conditions will prioritize health, safety, and hygiene.

16. Food Safety & Perishable Product Considerations

Our traditional homemade pickles, murabbas, laddus, and traditional preserves are consumable food products sealed in protective packaging. For hygiene and food safety compliance under Indian food regulations, opened or tampered food items cannot be accepted for return.

17. Non-Returnable & Restricted Items

[Pending Owner & Legal Counsel Approval] Specific categories of perishable food preserves designated as non-returnable once dispatched will be finalized with legal counsel prior to commercial launch.

18. Damaged, Defective, Missing, or Incorrect Items

When commercial sales launch, procedures for addressing glass jar transit breakage, defective seals, missing items, or incorrect product shipments will be established. Prompt notification through our Contact Page will be required.

19. Evidence Guidelines

Uploads Currently Unavailable: Customer image and document upload features are deferred and inactive. When reporting transit issues or inquiries, customers should describe the situation via text through our official Contact page.

20. Future Replacement & Exchange Handling

Replacement policies for items damaged during courier transit (such as shipping a fresh replacement jar upon verified transit breakage) will be published prior to commercial sales activation.

21. Future Refund Eligibility

Refund eligibility criteria (e.g. for verified seller-canceled orders or untreatable transit damage) will be established upon commercial sales launch.

22. Future Refund Method

Upon commercial activation, approved refunds will be credited back to the original source payment method (credit/debit card, UPI, net banking) utilized during checkout via integrated payment gateways.

23. Future Refund-Processing Timelines

Bank and payment gateway processing timelines for approved refunds (e.g. 5-7 business days) will be published when online payments are enabled. No active refund timelines exist while commerce is disabled.

24. Original Shipping or Delivery Charges

Rules regarding whether original shipping fees are refundable in the event of partial returns or courier delivery failures will be published prior to commercial sales.

25. Failed or Unsuccessful Delivery

Procedures for handling shipments returned due to incorrect recipient addresses or unreachability will be established upon commercial activation.

26. Refused Deliveries

Handling terms for parcels refused at the time of delivery without valid cause will be specified prior to commercial sales launch.

27. Customer-Provided Incorrect Information

Satvik Swaad is not responsible for delivery failures resulting from incorrect PIN codes or phone numbers provided by the customer.

28. Marketplace & Third-Party Purchases

Marketplace synchronization (`MARKETPLACE_AMAZON_ENABLED=false`) is currently disabled. Purchases made on external third-party platforms (if any) are governed exclusively by that third party's cancellation and refund policy.

29. Chargebacks & Payment Disputes

Because online payments are disabled, no credit card chargebacks or payment gateway disputes occur. When payments open, legitimate dispute resolution procedures will apply.

30. Abuse, Fraud & Repeated Misuse

We reserve the right to refuse cancellation or refund requests from accounts exhibiting fraudulent activity, false claims of damaged goods, or systematic policy abuse.

31. Consumer Rights & Statutory Protections

Nothing in this policy is intended to limit or exclude non-waivable statutory consumer rights guaranteed under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, or applicable Indian laws.

32. Relationship with Terms and Conditions

This policy operates in conjunction with our Terms and Conditions. In the event of any conflict regarding general website usage, the Terms and Conditions shall prevail.

33. Relationship with Shipping & Delivery Policy

Physical shipping aspects of order fulfillment are governed by our Shipping and Delivery Policy.

34. Relationship with Privacy Policy

Personal information collected during support inquiries or future refund processing is governed by our Privacy Policy.

35. Service Interruptions & Exceptional Circumstances

When commercial sales launch, exception handling for force majeure events (e.g. natural disasters, courier strikes, government lockdowns) will be evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

36. Changes to this Policy

We reserve the right to update this Cancellation and Refund Policy to reflect operational updates or launch readiness. Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

37. Contact & Complaint Method

If you have questions, feedback, or complaints regarding this policy or platform availability, please contact us through our official Contact Page.

38. Legal & Operational Review Notice

[Pending Owner & Legal Counsel Approval] Specific refund timelines, cancellation charges, and food return exclusions are subject to final operational approval and formal legal review prior to commercial launch.
