Artisanal Food Heritage & Policy Scope

Welcome to Satvik Swaad ("we", "our", or "us"). We craft authentic, artisanal homemade Indian pickles (Achar), murabbas, and traditional sweet delicacies using pure cold-pressed Kachi Ghani mustard oil, multi-day sun curing, and ancestral family recipes.

Every product is prepared in small micro-batches without chemical preservatives (zero Sodium Benzoate, zero artificial vinegar) and sealed into premium food-grade airtight glass jars. This policy governs all transactions and order fulfillment placed through our official website (satwikspot.web.app) and our authorized WhatsApp commerce channel.

2

Strict No-Refund & No-Return Policy (Consumable Goods)

⚠️ Food Safety & FSSAI Hygiene Protocol: In compliance with standard food safety regulations and sanitary hygiene protocols for consumable products, all sales are final once delivered. We strictly do not accept product returns, nor do we issue monetary/cash refunds for delivered food items.

Because pickles and traditional sweets are edible, temperature-sensitive goods packaged in glass containers, any item that has left our fulfillment facility and been delivered cannot be restocked, repurposed, or resold to another consumer.

Taste preferences, subjective flavour palate differences (such as natural spice level, saltiness, or traditional mustard oil pungency), and accidental multi-jar purchases by the customer are not eligible for returns or refunds.

3

Transit Damage & Glass-Jar Breakage Protection Guarantee

🛡️ 100% Free Replacement Guarantee: While we do not issue cash refunds, we take full responsibility for the safe arrival of our glass jars. If your package arrives physically broken, cracked, or leaking due to mishandling during courier transit, we provide an immediate free replacement at zero cost to you.

We employ custom heavy-duty corrugated cartons, bubble wrap, and tamper-evident seals to protect your jars. However, in the rare event of transit damage by third-party logistics carriers, your purchase is fully insured by Satvik Swaad through our Free Replacement policy.

4

Damage Reporting Window & Evidence Guidelines (24–48 Hours)

To claim a free replacement for a broken or leaking jar, the issue must be reported to us within 24 to 48 hours of confirmed delivery. Requests raised beyond 48 hours cannot be verified against logistics liability and will not be eligible.

Required Evidence for Verification:

  • Clear Photo / Unboxing Video: Showing the damaged or broken glass jar, along with the leaked oil/contents.
  • Outer Packaging Photo: Clearly displaying the courier shipping label with your Air Waybill (AWB) number and delivery address.
  • Order Identification: Your Order ID (e.g. SS-1042) and registered phone number.

Submit the evidence directly via WhatsApp at +91 92365 87600 or by email to satvikswaad.care@gmail.com. Our support team reviews and approves eligible cases within 2 to 4 business hours.

5

Incorrect or Missing Item Resolution

If you receive an incorrect product, weight variant, or an item from your order is missing:

  • Contact us within 24 hours of delivery with a photo of the parcel contents and the enclosed packing slip.
  • Upon quick verification, we will immediately dispatch the correct or missing item to your address at our own expense.
  • For food hygiene reasons, you are not required to ship back the incorrect consumable jar.
6

Order Cancellation Before Dispatch (Within 2 Hours)

Customers can cancel an order free of charge if the cancellation request is received within 2 hours of order placement, or before the package has been prepared and handed over to our courier partner (whichever occurs earlier).

To cancel an order before dispatch, immediately message our WhatsApp helpline at +91 92365 87600 with the text: "Cancel Order #[OrderID]".

If the cancelled order was prepaid, a 100% full reversal of the charged amount will be credited back to your original payment method within 3–5 working days.

7

Non-Cancellable Post-Dispatch Status

📦 Orders in Transit Cannot Be Cancelled: Once an order has been packed, an Air Waybill (AWB) has been generated, and the courier carrier has picked up the consignment, the shipment is in active movement across regional distribution hubs. At this stage, the order cannot be modified, cancelled, or intercepted.

Tracking notifications are sent automatically via SMS and WhatsApp once dispatch takes place so you can monitor real-time delivery milestones.

8

Seller-Initiated Cancellations & Full Reimbursements

Satvik Swaad reserves the right to cancel an order prior to dispatch under exceptional circumstances:

  • A fresh artisanal batch fails our internal sensory, aroma, or quality control audit.
  • The destination delivery pin-code is temporarily unserviceable or subject to logistics force majeure (severe weather, civil curfew, transport strikes).
  • Unexpected inventory depletion during peak festive rushes (e.g. Diwali, Holi).

In all seller-initiated cancellations of prepaid orders, a 100% full reimbursement is automatically processed back to the customer's source account within 3 to 5 business days, accompanied by an explanatory notification.

9

Customer Refusals & Undeliverable Shipments

Our delivery partners make up to three (3) delivery attempts before marking a consignment as Return to Origin (RTO).

  • Customer Unavailability / Incorrect Address: If an order cannot be delivered due to an incorrect or incomplete shipping address, invalid contact phone number, or unanswered delivery calls, the consignment will be returned to our facility.
  • Unjustified Delivery Refusal: Refusing delivery of an intact parcel without an authorized transit breakage claim results in total forfeiture of the order value, as perishable food items returned through reverse logistics cannot be resold.
  • Re-dispatch: If a customer requests re-delivery to an updated address after an RTO, standard reshipment courier costs will apply.
10

Prepaid Payment Gateway Security & Banking Reversals

Online prepaid transactions are securely processed through Razorpay, utilizing 256-bit SSL encryption and dual-factor authentication conforming to RBI regulations (supporting UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, RuPay, Visa, Mastercard, and Net Banking).

💳 Payment Deducted but Order Not Generated? If funds are debited from your bank or UPI app during checkout but network disruption prevents generation of an order confirmation, your funds are safe in the banking reconciliation pool. The payment gateway and your issuing bank automatically reconcile and reverse the amount to your source account within 3 to 7 banking days.
11

Cash on Delivery (COD) Terms & Conditions

To provide maximum convenience across India, we offer Cash on Delivery (COD) for serviceable pin codes.

  • COD payments must be settled in full (via cash or courier-assisted QR/UPI) before the courier executive opens the sealed parcel.
  • Because our pickles are freshly cured in small batches and packaged in glass, unauthorized COD rejections incur significant transit and food wastage. Accounts with repeated unwarranted COD refusals will have COD privileges permanently disabled.
12

Replacement Processing & Dispatch Timelines (24–48h)

Once our customer support team verifies your transit damage or incorrect item claim:

  • Your replacement jar is individually inspected, carefully re-packed in extra shock-resistant packaging, and dispatched within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Fresh Air Waybill (AWB) tracking information is automatically shared with you via SMS and WhatsApp.
  • Standard delivery timelines (3 to 6 business days depending on location) apply from the date of replacement dispatch.
