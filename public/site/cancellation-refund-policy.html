100% Free Replacement Guarantee for Glass-Jar Transit Damage

🛡️ 100% Free Replacement Guarantee: We take complete responsibility for the safe transit of our glass jars. If your package arrives physically cracked, broken, or leaking due to courier mishandling, we dispatch an immediate brand-new replacement at zero cost to you.

Every jar of Satvik Swaad pickles and traditional preserves is packaged in premium food-grade glass containers, cushioned in shock-absorbent multi-layer corrugated boxes, and sealed with tamper-evident tape. In the rare event of rough transit handling by third-party logistics carriers, you are completely protected.

Perishable Food Damage Reporting Protocol (Within 2 to 4 Hours of Delivery):

Because our artisanal delicacies are freshly prepared, chemical-free perishable food items packaged in glass jars, any transit damage, cracked container, or seal leakage must be reported within 2 to 4 hours of delivery with unboxing photo/video proof.

  • Step 1 (Photo / Video Proof): Take a clear photo or unboxing video highlighting the broken, cracked, or leaking glass jar and its contents.
  • Step 2 (Shipping Label): Take a clear photo of the outer cardboard box showing the courier shipping label with the Air Waybill (AWB) number.
  • Step 3 (Share via WhatsApp or Email): Send the photos along with your Order ID to our WhatsApp helpline at +91 92365 87600 or by email to satvikswaad.care@gmail.com.

Our support team verifies claims within 2 to 4 business hours. Upon approval, an individually inspected replacement jar is freshly packed and dispatched within 24 to 48 hours, with new tracking details sent straight to your phone. If a replacement is unavailable, an immediate full refund is credited back to your original source within 5 to 7 business days.

2

Order Cancellation Before Dispatch (Within 2 Hours) & Full Refund

⏱️ Easy Pre-Dispatch Cancellation: You can cancel your order free of charge if requested within 2 hours of order placement, or anytime before the package is handed over to our courier partner.

To cancel an order before dispatch, message our WhatsApp helpline at +91 92365 87600 with the text: "Cancel Order #[OrderID]".

  • 100% Full Refund for Prepaid Orders: If your cancelled order was prepaid via UPI, debit/credit card, or net banking, a 100% reimbursement is credited back to your original payment source within 5 to 7 business days via PayU.
  • Post-Dispatch In-Transit Status: Once an order has been packed, assigned an Air Waybill (AWB), and collected by our logistics partner, it is in active movement across regional distribution hubs. At this stage, shipments cannot be recalled, rerouted, or cancelled mid-transit.
  • Seller-Initiated Cancellations: If an artisanal batch does not meet our strict quality or sensory benchmarks, or if your delivery pincode experiences temporary carrier disruption, we will proactively notify you and process an automatic 100% full refund immediately within 5 to 7 business days.
3

Consumable Food Safety & Non-Returnable Hygiene Protocol

🚫 Non-Returnable Consumable Food Jars: In strict compliance with FSSAI hygiene standards, consumable food products (pickles, murabbas, and traditional sweets) cannot be physically returned or restocked once delivered.

Because food items are sensitive to temperature and handling conditions once unsealed, returning food parcels through postal channels compromises quality. We safeguard every household's kitchen by ensuring that no product dispatched from our unit is ever a returned or previously handled item.

4

Incorrect Variant or Missing Jar Resolution

Every package leaves our kitchen after a two-person verification checklist. In the unlikely circumstance that an item is missing or an incorrect jar variant was delivered:

  • Immediate Express Dispatch: Notify us within 24 hours of delivery. We will immediately dispatch the missing or correct jar via priority air express at zero expense to you.
  • Zero Return Hassle: For hygiene reasons, you are never required to ship back the incorrect food jar. You may keep it and enjoy it with our compliments.
5

Prepaid Payment Security & 5–7 Days Banking Reversals

Online prepaid transactions are securely routed through authorized payment gateways including PayU, utilizing 256-bit SSL encryption conforming to RBI regulations (supporting UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, RuPay, Visa, Mastercard, and Net Banking).

💳 Payment Reversal & Refund SLA: If an order is cancelled prior to dispatch or a refund is authorized for damaged perishable goods, your payment is credited directly to your original payment method (Bank Account / Credit Card / UPI) within 5 to 7 business days via PayU.
