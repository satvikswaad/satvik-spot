Frequently Asked Questions

What products does Satvik Swaad offer?
Satvik Swaad currently offers traditional pickles and amla-based products, including murabba, laddus, chyawanprash and amla juice. Visit the Products page for the current catalogue of 15 authentic items.
Where can I view all available products?
The complete current catalogue, available sizes, prices, and stock status can be viewed directly on our dedicated Products page.
Are all products always available?
Because our products are crafted in small artisanal batches using seasonal ingredients (such as fresh raw mangoes and wild amla), availability may vary by product and batch. The latest availability status is always shown on each product listing.
Can I place an order online right now?
Online ordering will open after required registrations and launch preparations are completed. You can browse all 15 products and add items to your cart to calculate totals in the meantime.
How can I check my order status?
Once orders are placed, order status can be tracked using your unique Order ID and guest access secret saved at checkout.
Can I change or cancel an order?
To request changes or cancellation, please contact customer support immediately. Cancellation or modification depends on whether the batch has already been packaged for dispatch.
How should I store pickles?
Follow the instructions printed on the actual product packaging: always use a clean, dry spoon, close the container properly after each use, keep the layer of mustard oil level, and store in a cool, dry place away from moisture.
How should amla products be stored?
Follow each product's packaging instructions because storage requirements can differ by product (e.g. amla juice should be refrigerated after opening, whereas amla murabba should be kept sealed in a cool place).
Where can I find ingredient and allergen information?
Ingredients and allergen information are listed on individual product cards and printed on product packaging labels.
What is the product shelf life?
Shelf life varies by product and batch. Customers should check the packaging label and product details for exact best-before information.
Where does Satvik Swaad deliver?
Delivery serviceability is verified at checkout based on pincode serviceability data provided during launch.
How long does delivery take?
Delivery times depend on the destination pincode and current batch preparation schedules, typically ranging from 3 to 7 business days.
Which payment methods are accepted?
While online payments are gated during launch prep, supported payment methods include Cash on Delivery (COD), UPI (GPay/PhonePe/Paytm), and Credit/Debit Cards.
Is my information secure?
Yes, your information is protected through server-validated, authenticated systems and treated in accordance with our Privacy Policy.
