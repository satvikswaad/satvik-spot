At Satvik Swaad, we believe in pure taste and pure privacy. We use only minimal, essential browser cookies to ensure your cart persists and your login is safe — with zero third-party advertising trackers.

Essential Functions Only

We only store data strictly necessary for your visit: saving pickles in your shopping cart, preserving your secure login, and remembering your language preference.

No Meta pixel, no Google AdSense, no behavioral retargeting. We are an artisanal kitchen, not an advertising broker. We never sell or share your activity.

Full Customer Control

You can view, clear, or block cookies anytime via your browser settings. Clearing cookies simply empties your cart and logs you out gracefully.

1

What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

When you browse any modern website, your web browser receives small pieces of text or data called Cookies and Local Storage (`localStorage`). These safe, client-side data containers allow websites to remember important actions you took, so you do not have to re-enter your information as you navigate between pages.

Just as our artisanal preserves are made with pure, unadulterated ingredients, our website is built with clean, unadulterated technology. We do not use intrusive tracking scripts, invasive fingerprinting, or deceptive advertising cookies.

2

We only use first-party, technically essential items. Without these, basic e-commerce functions such as carrying items to checkout or signing into your account would cease to function properly.

3

Zero Third-Party Advertising & Tracking

Many commercial e-commerce platforms embed third-party surveillance scripts (such as Facebook/Meta Pixel, Google AdSense, affiliate tracking networks, and cross-site data brokers) that spy on what you look at and follow you across the internet.

Satvik Swaad strictly prohibits third-party advertising trackers.

  • No Ad Pixels: We do not track you across other websites or social platforms.
  • No Behavioral Profiling: We do not build behavioural dossiers or sell your browsing history to advertising data brokers.
  • No Commercial Monetization: We earn our living purely by handcrafting exceptional pickles and sweets — never by monetizing customer data.
✨ Pure Artisanal Philosophy: We respect your home and digital sanctity just as we honor traditional kitchen purity. Your trust is our greatest asset.
4

How You Can Manage, Inspect, or Disable Cookies

You have absolute sovereignty over your browser data. You can inspect, delete, or block cookies and local storage through your browser settings at any time:

🌐 Google Chrome

Go to Settings › Privacy and Security › Third-Party Cookies or Site Settings to view and remove stored data.

🍏 Apple Safari

Go to Preferences / Settings › Privacy › Manage Website Data to remove or block stored cookies and storage.

🦊 Mozilla Firefox

Navigate to Settings › Privacy & Security › Cookies and Site Data and choose Clear Data.

💻 Microsoft Edge

Open Settings › Cookies and site permissions › Manage and delete cookies and site data.

⚠️ Please Note: If you completely block essential local storage in your browser, your shopping cart will not be able to save your selected items as you navigate between product pages, and login functionality will be disabled.
